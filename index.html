About

I’m Praveen, a Senior Researcher at the University of Oxford working on machine learning for healthcare: clinical language models, genomics, electronic health records, and medical imaging. Most of my day goes into making models a bit more useful, reliable, and helpful in real-world clinical settings.

I did my PhD at the HITLab in New Zealand and my Master’s at IIT Kharagpur. Before moving to the UK, I spent close to six years at M42 in Abu Dhabi, most recently as Principal Scientist. Outside of work, I enjoy hiking, travelling, and training Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.
